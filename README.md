# AI Physiotherapy Assistant

Final project for the Building AI course

## Summary

AI Physiotherapy Assistant is an AI-based system that helps physiotherapists monitor patients during rehabilitation. It analyzes body movements and helps track patient progress.

## Background

Many people need physiotherapy after injuries, operations, or muscle problems.

However, some patients perform their exercises incorrectly or forget to follow their rehabilitation programs.

This project aims to help physiotherapists monitor patients and understand their progress.

My motivation is my interest in physiotherapy and artificial intelligence. I believe AI can make rehabilitation easier and more effective.

## How is it used?

1. The patient performs rehabilitation exercises in front of a camera.
2. The system analyzes body movements using computer vision.
3. AI compares the movements with reference exercise patterns.
4. The system provides feedback about movement quality.
5. The physiotherapist reviews the results and follows the patient's progress.

The system supports physiotherapists and does not replace them.

## Data sources and AI methods

The project could use:

- Videos of rehabilitation exercises.
- Human body movement data.
- Exercise instructions.
- Patient progress records, with permission.

Possible AI methods:

- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Computer Vision
- Pose Estimation
- Classification

## Challenges

- Different patients have different physical abilities.
- Camera quality and lighting can affect results.
- AI may not recognize every movement correctly.
- Patient data must be protected.
- A physiotherapist must make the final clinical decisions.

The system cannot diagnose diseases or replace professional medical assessment.

## What next?

In the future, the project could:

- Support more rehabilitation exercises.
- Work with mobile phones.
- Track patient progress over time.
- Support home rehabilitation programs.
- Help physiotherapists create personalized exercise plans.
